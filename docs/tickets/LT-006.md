# LT-006 — Curate a sourced song journey and learning links

[Live kanban card](https://app.notion.com/p/3d907031acb581b583aee84f91e74c94?pvs=204) · Wave 1 · B — Follow a song

## Objective
Make the first journey informative enough to reward curiosity and trustworthy enough to ship.
## Scope
Research and author concise original summaries for producing and releasing a song. Target 5–7 stages, 6–10 roles and 8–12 related concepts/skills/tools; keep roughly 30–50 browsable entities. Include at least three learning resources across two concepts.
## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] Every displayed stage has a contextual contribution; entries say what the person does and why it matters in this process.
- [ ] Include a musically plausible multi-hop path such as production → mixing → sound/signal concepts → learning, grounded by sources rather than the ideation example alone.
- [ ] Every substantive relationship/claim has relevant source metadata; record checked date and evidence scope, distinguish editorial process framing from universal fact.
- [ ] Learning resources have verified live URLs, provider/title, learner level and separate access/license labels. At least one is suitable for a beginner/high-school learner.
- [ ] No fabricated local-presence, salary, degree requirements or historical claims. Avoid copying third-party prose or unlicensed assets; report gaps instead of meeting a quota with invention.
- [ ] PM/content review checks source support; LT-005 validation must pass before release integration.
## Dependencies
[LT-002](LT-002.md)
## Verification
Open authoritative source/resource pages, sample the claim-to-source links, and run the release validator when LT-005 is available.
## Out of scope
No full occupation profiles, scraped course catalog, salaries or geographic market comparison.
## Handoff
Ticket status and acceptance evidence are tracked in Notion; these unchecked boxes define the contract, not a live progress mirror.
Planned role: Content research. Size: L — up to 2 days. Estimates indicate focused engineering effort, not a calendar commitment.
When starting, set an actual Agent, move to In progress and name files owned. For review, attach exact changed files/commit, relevant check results and a visible preview when applicable. PM checks acceptance before Done.
