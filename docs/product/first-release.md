# First playable release

## Outcome
Help a curious high-school learner answer “How does this get done?”, discover an unfamiliar role through its contribution, and find an approachable next thing to learn. The first demo should make exploration feel inviting without requiring knowledge of graph terminology.
## Scope decision — 12 September 2026
Wave 1 is a complete song journey. Coffee from Uganda to a European café follows in Wave 2. Lincoln confirmed song first, coffee second on 12 September 2026. Preserve the other four examples as model stress tests and later content, rather than building six shallow demos.

[LT-020](../tickets/LT-020.md) supersedes the initial home/trail presentation: a global mixed tile wall with optional automatic rotation, explicit catalog search, sources/attribution hidden by default behind Settings, no visible trail and conventional activity names. ## Wave 1 experience
- Enter through a mixed Curiosity Wall or local search across the seeded endeavors, roles, knowledge and tools. “How does X get done?” leads the experience.
- Open producing and releasing a song and see a curated process overview.
- Select a stage, understand what specific roles contribute there, and follow a related skill, concept or tool.
- Re-center a bounded neighborhood as the learner follows a connection; keep orientation through back/home and focus state.
- Read a concise description of what someone does in context and why a connection exists. Keep source links inspectable through the detailed-information setting.
- Open at least three relevant, checked learning resources across at least two concepts. Label access separately from reuse license; include beginner relevance.
- Refresh a deep link and restore the selected node. An optional `?via=` contribution in the focal hash preserves context; browser history restores focal state without a visible trail. Copied URLs do not serialize the full trail. A local production preview must work before deployment.
## Content budget
One sourced endeavor; target 5–7 stages, 6–10 roles and 8–12 related knowledge/skill/tool entries, keeping the whole release roughly 30–50 browsable entities. These are scope ceilings and useful coverage targets, not quotas that justify invented content. Every displayed stage has at least one contextual contribution. At least one cross-connection supports an interesting multi-hop trail.
No seed salary numbers, local-presence claims or historical chronologies are to be copied from illustrative ideation examples. Geography and time have schema support in Wave 1; their user-facing lenses follow when sourced content exists.
## Design direction
Take inspiration from Windows Phone: strong type hierarchy, flat saturated color, asymmetric composition, generous space, edge-aligned layouts and purposeful movement. Use modern readable system fonts and accessible contrast; large light headlines can coexist with comfortably weighted body text.
Design a focused, spatial exploration with a small visible neighborhood, labeled connections and responsive detail panels. Avoid exposing the whole network at once. System and Team are initial ways of understanding the same endeavor; learning appears in context. Full semantic zoom and six persistent lenses are later scope.
At 360px viewport width, exploration must remain usable without page-level horizontal scrolling. Keyboard users can reach the same entities through a structured companion list. Reduced-motion preference removes nonessential transitions and tile animation. Focus must remain visible; color cannot be the only carrier of type or state.
## Checkpoints and success
**A — Visual foundation:** local runnable shell with discovery and explorer compositions, responsive at 360px and desktop, using clearly labeled fixture content. Show the direction for feedback before deep implementation.
**B — Follow a song:** real seed data powers endeavor → stage → contextual contribution/role → knowledge/tool traversal. Every transition can explain its connection; back and reset work.
**C — Discover and learn:** search, trail navigation, checked learning links, deep-link refresh and quality checks work together in a production build. Publish once GitHub setup is resolved; the local demo can be reviewed first.
Use a five-minute walkthrough: start from a song, find two distinct contributions, explain one relationship, reach a new concept, open a relevant learning resource and return without losing orientation. PM executes this before Done. Feedback from Lincoln and, when available, a sister is a discovery checkpoint, not a fabricated usability result or a prerequisite for routine implementation.
## Explicitly deferred
Accounts, cloud synchronization, generative chat, user-generated editing, recommendation/assessment engines, salary/outlook dashboards, comprehensive occupation imports, semantic zoom across the world, a historical time slider, shared trails, offline install/reopen support and monetization.
Wave 2 adds a sourced coffee journey and evidence-aware World/Africa/Kenya/Uganda emphasis. Kenya and Uganda are siblings under Africa; unverified presence means unknown, not nonexistence. Local bookmark persistence is separately prioritized after the core loop works.
## Data principles
Separate artifacts from the endeavors that produce them. Roles are broader than occupations. Contributions bind a role’s activity to an endeavor/stage, so collaboration can be derived in context. Keep knowledge, skill and tools distinct enough to make useful navigation possible.
Use stable IDs, explicit relationship meanings, claim-level provenance and checked-at dates. Keep optional temporal qualifiers and geography evidence without manufacturing certainty. Original summaries link to sources; reuse rights are recorded for imported material.
